The rental includes: vehicle with owner's comprehensive insurance, written agreement, 24/7 support, and professional ISO-certified coordination. Fuel, parking, and tolls are separate. Final price depends on vehicle condition and owner's rate.
Is insurance included?
All vehicles come with comprehensive insurance maintained by car owners. We verify coverage before booking. PwaniRides does not provide additional insurance - coverage comes from the vehicle owner.

Prices start from KES 3,000/day. Final rates depend on vehicle condition and are set by car owners. Contact us for exact pricing on available vehicles.
For car owners: When do I get paid?
You receive payment immediately when the client confirms booking by paying before vehicle handover. No waiting periods or delays.
