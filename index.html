Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What's included in the rental price?
The rental price includes: verified vehicle with insurance coverage, written rental agreement, 24/7 WhatsApp support, free Mombasa itinerary guide, and unlimited mileage within the Coast region. Fuel, parking, and tolls are not included.
Do I need to pay a deposit?
Yes, a refundable security deposit is required (amount varies by vehicle). This protects the car owner and is fully refunded after you return the vehicle in the same condition. All deposit terms are clearly documented in the rental agreement.
Is insurance included?
All vehicles we list come from car owners who maintain comprehensive insurance. Before your rental, we verify that valid insurance is in place. However, you should review the insurance details and consider additional coverage if needed for your peace of mind.
Can I drive outside Mombasa?
Yes! You can drive anywhere in the Coast region (Mombasa, Kilifi, Diani, Malindi, Watamu) with unlimited mileage. For trips to Nairobi or other regions, please inform us in advance so we can confirm with the car owner.
What if I have issues during my rental?
We provide 24/7 WhatsApp support (0701190155). Whether it's a flat tire, getting lost, or needing restaurant recommendations, message us anytime. We're local and always available to help.
For car owners: How much can I earn?
Earnings depend on your vehicle type and availability. Economy cars rent for KES 3,000-4,000/day, SUVs for KES 5,000+/day. You receive 70-80% of the daily rate. If your car is rented 10 days/month at KES 3,500/day, you'd earn approximately KES 24,500-28,000/month.
For car owners: What if a renter damages my car?
This is why we require you to maintain comprehensive insurance coverage. We also collect a security deposit from renters, screen all clients thoroughly, and document vehicle condition before/after each rental. Everything is in writing to protect you.
How do I know this isn't a scam?
Great question. Here's how we build trust: (1) Everything is documented in writing, (2) We verify all parties before any transaction, (3) We're transparent about our process publicly, (4) We use business WhatsApp and have a registered presence, (5) We encourage you to speak with previous clients. We're local, accessible, and building this business for the long term.
